Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

What Content OS accesses

  • Google Drive: files in the Drive folders the account owner uses for Content OS (stream recordings, finished videos and reports). It reads and writes only what the tool needs for that work.
  • YouTube: for a creator who has authorized it, it uploads Shorts to and edits descriptions on that creator's own channel.

What it does with that data

  • The data is used only to make and publish the creator's own videos.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored on the operator's own computer and are never published.

Your control

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask a question or have data removed, open an issue on this site's repository.
